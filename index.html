🛠️ DIY Circular Knitting Machine Masterclass

Step 1: Cylinder Prep

Sawing Vertical Slots

Prepare your main knitting cylinder using a smooth plastic pipe.

  • 1 Cut a 3-inch UPVC pipe to exactly 12 cm in length.
  • 2 Wrap a paper template around the top rim to mark 24 even slots.
  • 3 Saw straight vertical slots down to 2 mm depth.
💡 Pro Tip: Lightly smooth inside each slot with sandpaper so yarn glides freely without snagging!
3D Diagram: Slotted Cylinder
Step 2: Wavy Cam Track

Making the Wave Channel

Build the special wavy track that guides needle movement!

  • 1 Draw a uniform sine-wave pattern around a wider PVC sleeve.
  • 2 Cut along the wave line to split it into upper and lower parts.
  • 3 Mount both halves leaving a precise 4 mm track gap.
💡 Pro Tip: Test with a needle butt to make sure it glides smoothly through the gap without jamming.
2D Diagram: Cam Track Channel
Step 3: Base Rig

Wooden Stand Assembly

Mount the cylinder onto a sturdy desk stand that spins smoothly.

  • 1 Cut a flat square wooden board (10x10 inches) for stability.
  • 2 Attach a circular wooden ring in the center to seat the cylinder.
  • 3 Apply wax along the contact ring for low-friction rotation.
💡 Pro Tip: Standard candle wax works as a great, safe lubricant instead of messy oil!
3D Diagram: Base Stand
Step 4: Kinematic Motion

Needles & Crank Action

Assemble latch needles and rotate the cylinder to knit tubular fabric!

  • 1 Place latch needles into every vertical slot.
  • 2 Align needle metal butts inside the wavy outer track.
  • 3 Thread yarn through the top feeder and rotate to knit!
